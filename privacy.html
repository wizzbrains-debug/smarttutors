Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0 (Subject to final human legal review)

Independent Educational Platform Notice SmartTutors is an independent educational platform. SmartTutors is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE), Pearson Edexcel, or any official examination board.

1. Overview & Commitment to Student Privacy

SmartTutors values the privacy of students, guardians, and visitors. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and process personal and academic information when you use our educational website and Socratic AI tutoring assistant.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information necessary to deliver educational tutoring and manage daily usage allowances:

  • Student Account Data: Full name, email address, password hash (processed securely through Supabase Auth), chosen qualification track (Cambridge O-Level or A-Level), and optional mobile number.
  • Terms & Consent Audit Records: Explicit timestamp records confirming acceptance of service terms and marketing communication preferences.
  • AI Tutoring Inquiries: The text of student questions, subject selections, and immediate conversation history required to provide contextual Socratic hints.
  • Usage Accounting Data: Daily question usage counts per student ID, used solely to enforce the 5 free questions/day allowance resetting at midnight PKT.
  • Guest Identifiers: Ephemeral, HMAC-signed session cookies (e.g. st_guest_id) and their SHA-256 hashes used to track trial usage without collecting personal identity.

3. How Information Is Used

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate students and secure their account sessions.
  • To generate tailored Socratic learning hints based on Cambridge syllabus topics.
  • To enforce daily question limits fairly across all learners.
  • To send syllabus updates, revision materials, and Cambridge exam preparation guidance only if the student has explicitly opted into marketing communications.

We do not sell student personal information, nor do we share it with third parties for behavioral advertising.

4. Technical Security & Database Safeguards

SmartTutors employs architectural defense-in-depth measures:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Students can only view their own account profile and usage history.
  • PostgreSQL Column-Level Grants: Database permissions restrict authenticated students to modifying non-sensitive fields (name, mobile, qualification, marketing consent). Protected columns such as terms acceptance timestamps and minor status cannot be modified via client scripts.
  • Strict Secret Isolation: Administrative service-role database keys are strictly isolated to serverless execution environments and are never transmitted to client browsers.
  • Token Rotation: Client sessions use short-lived JWT access tokens with proactive mutex-guarded refresh rotation.

5. Minor Students & Guardian Awareness

SmartTutors serves secondary education students preparing for Cambridge O-Level and A-Level examinations, many of whom are minors.

We treat minor student data with heightened care: accounts record minor status indicators, and guardian consent records are supported in the database schema. Parents or guardians may review or request deletion of their child’s account data at any time by contacting our academic support team.

6. Cookies & Browser Storage

Our platform uses minimal browser storage:

  • Local Storage: Stores active session access tokens and UI preferences (e.g. dark mode toggle).
  • Secure Cookies: Guest trial question allowances use secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax HMAC-signed cookies. No third-party tracking cookies are set.

7. User Rights & Profile Management

Students can view their profile, change their qualification track, and toggle marketing preferences at any time directly through the Student Account drawer.

To request full deletion of your student account and associated data, please contact our support team.

8. Contact & Legal Review Notice

For privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact us via our Contact Page.
